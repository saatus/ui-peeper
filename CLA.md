# Contributor License Agreement

## In plain English

This is the standard bargain that lets an open-source project stay maintainable:

- **You keep the copyright in your work.** This is a licence, not a transfer. You can
  still use, publish and relicense your own contribution however you like.
- **You give the project permission to use it**, including the right to sublicense.
  That is what allows the project to be relicensed later — for example to offer a
  commercial licence alongside AGPL — without needing to find and get written
  permission from every person who ever sent a patch.
- **You confirm the work is actually yours to give**, and that your employer doesn't
  own it in a way that conflicts.

The legal text below is what actually binds. It follows the structure of the widely
used Apache Individual Contributor License Agreement v2.0.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. A bot will comment asking you to sign, and you sign by posting a new
comment on your pull request containing exactly this line:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature is recorded in `signatures/version1/cla.json` in this repository. You only
sign once — later pull requests are checked against that file automatically.
